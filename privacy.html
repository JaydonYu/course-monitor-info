Privacy policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the owner-operated Canvas Course Monitor and its planned Google Drive export integration.

Data accessed and purpose

The monitor uses authorized Canvas access to collect course and assignment metadata, dates, change indicators and submission-status evidence for personal coursework monitoring. It does not submit coursework or modify Canvas course records.

After Google authorization, the Drive integration uses the drive.file scope to create and update designated export files. It accesses those files' content and metadata, such as identifiers and modification information, to synchronize and verify the exports. It does not scan the owner's entire Drive.

Storage and credentials

Collector records are stored on the owner's private cloud server. Derived export files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are used by the authorization helper and server to maintain the authorized connection. Credentials and course data must not be placed in this public website or its repository.

Sharing and use

The monitor does not sell data or use it for advertising or model training. The hosting provider and Google process data as needed to provide their respective services. The owner may separately authorize a personal assistant, including ChatGPT, to read the Drive exports; that service's handling of the information is governed by its own terms and the owner's settings. This website does not automatically send coursework data to an assistant.

Drive export files are intended to remain private unless the owner changes their sharing settings.

Retention and deletion

Export snapshots are replaced during successful synchronization. Local collector records and Drive files remain until removed by the owner or an applicable retention setting. Revoking authorization does not itself delete previously stored files.

The owner can stop the synchronization service, remove the app's access through Google Account connections, delete the export files from Drive, and delete local collector records and saved credentials.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics code, tracking cookies, forms or sign-in functionality. The website hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.

Google API data use

The monitor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions and changes

Contact the project maintainer using the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen. This page will be updated before material changes to the described data practices.
